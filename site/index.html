What does your vehicle really cost you?

AutoLedger adds up everything a vehicle costs, to the cent: fuel and electricity, financing, depreciation, tires, tolls, maintenance and insurance. Electric, hybrid or combustion, on your own server.

Features

One ledger, every cost

One cost ledger feeds every figure you see, so the cost per km on the dashboard is the sum of what you actually paid.

A full TCO engine

Cash purchase, loan or lease with fees, deposit, mileage allowance and resale value. Real cost per km, net of carpooling revenue, with a completeness score. Battery health and a resale value projection come from your own figures.

Automatic upload

Let your car report itself

If your vehicle already reports to Home Assistant, the AutoLedger module turns those readings into cost entries. It reads the entities you choose, so it works with any integration that exposes them.

1. Your vehicle integration

A Home Assistant integration for your brand exposes the odometer, the battery level and the charging state as entities.

2. The AutoLedger module

Installed through HACS, it watches your wallbox or energy meter, merges the pauses caused by passing clouds into one session, and picks up the odometer.

3. Your ledger

Each charging session is priced with your tariff and added to the right vehicle. The odometer keeps the cost per distance up to date.

Sent by the Home Assistant module

  • Charging sessions, from the energy counter of your wallbox or sub-meter. A wallbox or energy meter entity is required.
  • The odometer, when the vehicle integration exposes it.
  • The battery level at the start and end of a charge.

Through other sources

  • Trips and fill-ups through the ingestion API, from a script, a Home Assistant automation or an OBD2 dongle.
  • Trips, charges and odometer of Tesla vehicles through the TeslaMate synchronisation.
  • Anything else with a CSV import.

Tesla: two ways, different levels of detail

With TeslaMate

AutoLedger synchronises directly with TeslaMate: odometer, trips and charging sessions, with the detail TeslaMate records. This is the fullest option.

With the Tesla Fleet integration

The official Home Assistant integration feeds the AutoLedger module with the battery level, the energy added and the odometer, which is disabled by default. It does not provide trip history. Tesla bills access to its Fleet API.

Peugeot, Citroën, DS, Opel, Vauxhall

Stellantis Vehicles

The community integration reads the mileage, the battery level, the charging state, the last trip and the last charge, and the position. It needs the account of the brand’s mobile app. Waking the car remotely can drain the 12 V battery, so keep the refresh interval reasonable.

Quickstart

Up and running in two commands

Multi-architecture image (amd64 and arm64) with a bundled PostgreSQL and an optional backup service.

bash 
$ git clone https://github.com/Rem7474/AutoLedger.git && cd AutoLedger
$ docker compose up -d   # http://localhost:8080

No configuration needed: the database password and the session and encryption secrets are generated on first start and kept in Docker volumes, so back those up with your database. Set your own in .env if you prefer. Full variable reference and upgrade notes are in the README.
