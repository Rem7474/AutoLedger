What does your vehicle really cost you?

AutoLedger is a self-hosted ledger that adds up everything a vehicle costs, to the cent: fuel and electricity, financing, depreciation, tires, tolls, maintenance and insurance. Electric, hybrid or combustion, any brand, on your own server.

What it does

One cost ledger feeds every figure you see, so the cost per km on the dashboard is the sum of what you actually paid.

Full TCO engine

Cash purchase, loan or lease (LOA/LLD) with fees, deposit, mileage allowance and resale value. Real cost per km, net of carpooling revenue, with a completeness score.

Fuel and electricity

Fill-ups and charging sessions in one place: L/100 km or kWh/100 km, price paid per unit, and the real cost per km of each energy.

Electricity tariffs

For vehicles that charge: flat or time-of-use plans with public presets, applied to each session when it is recorded.

Tires by axle

Mount, swap and store tires per position, log tread depth and project the remaining mileage.

Carpooling

Split a trip fairly using the real energy price and a per-km insurance share.

Maintenance reminders

Date or mileage triggers, delivered to Discord, Telegram, Gotify or any JSON webhook such as Home Assistant, Node-RED or n8n.

Fleet and comparison

Several vehicles in one account, a fleet dashboard, and electric vs combustion scenarios built from your own data.

Documents

Attach invoices, receipts and registration cards to your records, stored on your own volume.

Secure by default

Local accounts or OIDC single sign-on (Authentik, Keycloak, Authelia, Kanidm), encrypted stored credentials, rate limiting and security headers.

Bring your data your way

The vehicle is described by what you actually have. Nothing is tied to one brand or one powertrain.

Manual PWA

Add a fill-up, a charge, a toll or an odometer reading in a few taps on your phone. Entries made offline are queued and sent when you are back online.

CSV import

Column mapping with preview, saved profiles, deduplication, and one-click undo of a whole import batch. Works for fill-ups, charges, trips and odometer readings.

Ingestion API and Home Assistant

Push drives, charges, fill-ups and odometer readings with scoped tokens, from an OBD2 logger, a script or Home Assistant. The official Home Assistant integration (HACS) detects wallbox sessions for you.

Telemetry sync

Optional continuous sync with TeslaMate for vehicles it supports: odometer, drives and charges.

Run it in two minutes

Multi-architecture image (amd64 and arm64) with a bundled PostgreSQL and optional backup service.

mkdir autoledger && cd autoledger
curl -O https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Rem7474/AutoLedger/main/docker-compose.yml
curl -o .env https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Rem7474/AutoLedger/main/.env.example
openssl rand -hex 32   # AUTOLEDGER_ENCRYPTION_KEY
docker compose up -d   # http://localhost:8080

Set AUTOLEDGER_ENCRYPTION_KEY, AUTOLEDGER_DB_PASSWORD and AUTOLEDGER_JWT_SECRET in .env first. Full variable reference and upgrade notes are in the README.
