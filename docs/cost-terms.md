# Cost and consumption terms

The dashboard, the fleet comparison and the month detail use the same terms. Each is defined once here and on hover in the app.

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Cash paid | Amounts actually paid, as recorded. Energy estimated for distance without drive data is included and always shown as "of which … estimated energy". |
| Economic cost | Costs spread over the period they cover instead of the day they were paid (amortized maintenance, tires and financing). |
| Full cost | The economic cost plus depreciation: what owning the vehicle really costs. |
| Running cost | Energy or fuel, tolls and parking only. |
| Consumption at the wheel | Energy used while driving, as measured by the vehicle (energy panel). |
| Consumption at the plug | Energy drawn when charging, losses included, over the distance driven (fleet comparison). |
| Estimated energy | Energy priced for distance driven without drive data, from the vehicle's average consumption and price. |

## Period of the dashboard figures

The cash paid card leads with the last 12 calendar months, the current month included, with the economic cost and its cost per distance over the same months; the value since the first record is secondary text. With less history the card names the months it covers. These figures are the sums of the bars of the monthly charts over that window.
